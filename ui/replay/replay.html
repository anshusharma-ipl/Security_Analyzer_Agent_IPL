What leaves this computer. Nothing, until you connect a model. With one connected, the code the agents read is sent to the provider you chose, and only to it. Secrets, emails and phone numbers are replaced with placeholders first, automatically. Your files are never changed; fixes are tried in a temporary copy and only written to a patch for you to apply. Use fake or non-sensitive code until your security team has approved the provider.
